<h1>App Submission Guidelines</h1>

<p class="guidelines-intro">Welcome to VibeStore! We're excited to help you share your app with our community. Please read these guidelines carefully before submitting your application.</p>

<hr>

<h2>What We're Looking For</h2>

VibeStore is a curated platform for discovering quality applications. We welcome submissions that:

<h3>✓ Provide Real Value</h3>
<ul>
<li>Solve a specific problem or fulfill a clear need</li>
<li>Offer unique features or approaches</li>
<li>Provide a good user experience</li>
<li>Are functional and ready for users</li>
</ul>

<h3>✓ Fit Our Categories</h3>
<p>We accept three types of applications:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Web Apps</strong> – Browser-based applications and services</li>
<li><strong>Mobile Apps</strong> – iOS and Android applications</li>
<li><strong>WhatsApp Apps</strong> – WhatsApp-based bots, services, and automation tools</li>
</ul>

<h3>✓ Meet Quality Standards</h3>
<ul>
<li>Functional links and working features</li>
<li>Accurate and honest descriptions</li>
<li>Appropriate and professional content</li>
<li>Clear value proposition</li>
<li>No misleading information</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>Before You Submit</h2>

<h3>1. Review Our Platform Disclaimer</h3>
<p><strong>Important:</strong> VibeStore is a <strong>sharing and discovery platform only</strong>. We:</p>
<ul>
<li>Do NOT develop, host, or own the listed applications</li>
<li>Do NOT have control over third-party app content or functionality</li>
<li>Do NOT take responsibility for what happens within listed applications</li>
<li>Do NOT guarantee the quality, safety, or availability of any app</li>
</ul>

<h3>2. Your Responsibilities as a Submitter</h3>
<p>By submitting an app, you confirm that:</p>
<ul>
<li>You have the right to submit this app (you're the creator, owner, or authorized representative)</li>
<li>All information provided is accurate and truthful</li>
<li>The app does not violate any laws or third-party rights</li>
<li>The app is functional and the link works correctly</li>
<li>You understand that VibeStore is not responsible for your app's content or practices</li>
</ul>

<h3>3. User Responsibilities</h3>
<p>Users who discover apps through our platform are solely responsible for:</p>
<ul>
<li>Evaluating the safety and suitability of apps before using them</li>
<li>Reading and understanding app terms and privacy policies</li>
<li>Any data they share with third-party applications</li>
<li>Any consequences from downloading or using listed apps</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>Submission Requirements</h2>

<h3>Required Information</h3>
<p>Every submission must include:</p>

<ol>
<li><strong>App Name</strong> – Clear and accurate title</li>
<li><strong>Description</strong> – Detailed explanation of features and benefits (100-500 characters)</li>
<li><strong>Working Link</strong> – Valid URL to the app or download page</li>
<li><strong>Category</strong> – Web, Mobile, or WhatsApp</li>
<li><strong>App Type</strong> – Specific category (e.g., Productivity, Entertainment, etc.)</li>
<li><strong>Your Email</strong> – For communication about your submission</li>
</ol>

<h3>Optional Information</h3>
<p>Enhance your listing with:</p>
<ul>
<li>Screenshots or images (where supported)</li>
<li>Pricing information (Free, Freemium, Paid)</li>
<li>Platform details (iOS, Android, Web, etc.)</li>
<li>Key features and highlights</li>
<li>Target audience</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>What We Accept</h2>

<h3>✓ Approved Content</h3>
<ul>
<li>Legitimate web applications and services</li>
<li>Mobile apps available on App Store or Google Play</li>
<li>WhatsApp business tools and automation services</li>
<li>Educational and productivity tools</li>
<li>Entertainment and lifestyle apps</li>
<li>E-commerce and shopping platforms</li>
<li>Communication and social tools</li>
<li>Development and technical tools</li>
<li>Creative and design applications</li>
<li>Health and wellness apps (with appropriate disclaimers)</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>What We Reject</h2>

<h3>✗ Prohibited Content</h3>
<p>We will reject or remove apps that:</p>

<h4>Illegal or Harmful</h4>
<ul>
<li>Promote illegal activities</li>
<li>Contain malware, viruses, or malicious code</li>
<li>Violate intellectual property rights</li>
<li>Distribute pirated or unauthorized content</li>
</ul>

<h4>Inappropriate Content</h4>
<ul>
<li>Promote hate speech, discrimination, or violence</li>
<li>Contain explicit adult content</li>
<li>Target or exploit minors</li>
<li>Include excessive violence or disturbing content</li>
</ul>

<h4>Low Quality or Spam</h4>
<ul>
<li>Non-functional or broken links</li>
<li>Duplicate submissions (we may merge similar apps)</li>
<li>Placeholder or incomplete apps</li>
<li>Spam or irrelevant content</li>
<li>Apps with no clear purpose or value</li>
</ul>

<h4>Deceptive Practices</h4>
<ul>
<li>Misleading descriptions or false claims</li>
<li>Phishing or scam applications</li>
<li>Fake reviews or manipulated ratings</li>
<li>Impersonation of other apps or brands</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>Review Process</h2>

<h3>How It Works</h3>

<ol>
<li><strong>Submit Your App</strong>
   <ul>
   <li>Fill out the submission form completely</li>
   <li>Provide accurate information</li>
   <li>Include all required fields</li>
   </ul>
</li>

<li><strong>Manual Review (2-5 Business Days)</strong>
   <ul>
   <li>Our team reviews each submission individually</li>
   <li>We verify links and basic functionality</li>
   <li>We check for guideline compliance</li>
   <li>We may edit descriptions for clarity and consistency</li>
   </ul>
</li>

<li><strong>Approval or Rejection</strong>
   <ul>
   <li><strong>Approved:</strong> Your app goes live on VibeStore</li>
   <li><strong>Rejected:</strong> You'll receive feedback via email</li>
   <li><strong>Needs Changes:</strong> We may request revisions</li>
   </ul>
</li>

<li><strong>Post-Approval</strong>
   <ul>
   <li>Your app appears in search results</li>
   <li>Users can review and rate your app</li>
   <li>You can update information via our system</li>
   </ul>
</li>
</ol>

<h3>Review Criteria</h3>
<p>We evaluate submissions based on:</p>
<ul>
<li>Functionality and quality</li>
<li>Guideline compliance</li>
<li>Value to users</li>
<li>Accuracy of information</li>
<li>Uniqueness (we prefer not to list duplicates)</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>After Approval</h2>

<h3>Maintaining Your Listing</h3>
<ul>
<li>Keep your app information up to date</li>
<li>Respond to user reviews professionally</li>
<li>Report broken links or issues promptly</li>
<li>Update pricing or feature changes</li>
</ul>

<h3>Moderation</h3>
<p>We reserve the right to:</p>
<ul>
<li>Edit listings for clarity and consistency</li>
<li>Remove apps that violate guidelines</li>
<li>Merge duplicate listings</li>
<li>Update categories or classifications</li>
<li>Remove non-functional apps</li>
</ul>

<h3>User Reviews</h3>
<ul>
<li>Users can rate and review your app</li>
<li>Only verified users (who clicked through to your app) can leave reviews</li>
<li>We moderate reviews to ensure quality and fairness</li>
<li>Manipulating reviews is strictly prohibited</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>Tips for a Successful Submission</h2>

<h3>Writing Great Descriptions</h3>
<ul>
<li>Be clear and concise</li>
<li>Highlight key features and benefits</li>
<li>Explain what problems your app solves</li>
<li>Use proper grammar and spelling</li>
<li>Avoid excessive marketing language</li>
</ul>

<h3>Choosing Categories</h3>
<ul>
<li>Select the most relevant primary category</li>
<li>Be specific about your app type</li>
<li>Consider how users will search for your app</li>
</ul>

<h3>Standing Out</h3>
<ul>
<li>Provide compelling, honest descriptions</li>
<li>Ensure your app offers real value</li>
<li>Keep your information current</li>
<li>Engage with user feedback</li>
</ul>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

<h3>How long does approval take?</h3>
<p>Typically 2-5 business days. Complex apps or high submission volumes may take longer.</p>

<h3>Can I submit someone else's app?</h3>
<p>Only if you're authorized (e.g., you're a team member, partner, or have permission). Unauthorized submissions will be rejected.</p>

<h3>What if my app is rejected?</h3>
<p>You'll receive feedback via email. Address the issues and resubmit if appropriate.</p>

<h3>Can I update my listing?</h3>
<p>Yes, contact us via the Report feature or your submission email to request updates.</p>

<h3>Do you accept paid apps?</h3>
<p>Yes, we accept free, freemium, and paid apps. Please indicate pricing clearly in your submission.</p>

<h3>What about duplicate apps?</h3>
<p>We prefer unique listings. Duplicates may be rejected or merged with existing entries.</p>

<h3>Can I promote my app?</h3>
<p>Basic listings are free. Contact us at vibeappstore@gmail.com to discuss promotional opportunities.</p>
