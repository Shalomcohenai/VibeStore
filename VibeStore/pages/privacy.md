<h1>Privacy Policy</h1>

<p class="privacy-intro">At VibeStore, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our platform and use our services.</p>

<div class="privacy-updated">Last updated: January 2025</div>

<hr>

<h2>1. Information We Collect</h2>

<h3>Personal Information</h3>
<p>We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:</p>
<ul>
<li>Create an account on our platform</li>
<li>Submit an app for listing</li>
<li>Subscribe to our newsletter</li>
<li>Leave reviews or ratings</li>
<li>Contact us for support or report issues</li>
<li>Participate in our community features</li>
</ul>

<p>This information may include:</p>
<ul>
<li>Name and email address</li>
<li>Authentication credentials (encrypted)</li>
<li>Profile information</li>
<li>App submission details and metadata</li>
<li>User preferences and favorites</li>
</ul>

<h3>Automatically Collected Information</h3>
<p>When you visit VibeStore, we automatically collect certain information about your device and usage patterns:</p>
<ul>
<li>IP address and browser type</li>
<li>Device information and operating system</li>
<li>Pages visited and time spent on our platform</li>
<li>Referring URLs and search terms</li>
<li>Click patterns and interaction data</li>
</ul>

<h3>Cookies and Tracking Technologies</h3>
<p>We use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to:</p>
<ul>
<li>Remember your preferences and settings</li>
<li>Analyze usage patterns and improve our services</li>
<li>Provide personalized content and recommendations</li>
<li>Measure the effectiveness of our features</li>
</ul>

<p>You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings, though some features may not function properly without cookies.</p>

<hr>

<h2>2. How We Use Your Information</h2>

<p>We use the collected information for the following purposes:</p>

<h3>Service Provision</h3>
<ul>
<li>Create and manage user accounts</li>
<li>Process and display app submissions</li>
<li>Enable favorites, lists, and personalization features</li>
<li>Facilitate user interactions (reviews, ratings)</li>
<li>Send transactional emails and notifications</li>
</ul>

<h3>Communication</h3>
<ul>
<li>Send newsletter updates (with your consent)</li>
<li>Respond to support requests and inquiries</li>
<li>Send important service announcements</li>
<li>Notify you of changes to our terms or policies</li>
</ul>

<h3>Analytics and Improvement</h3>
<ul>
<li>Analyze user behavior and usage patterns</li>
<li>Improve our search algorithms and recommendations</li>
<li>Identify and fix technical issues</li>
<li>Develop new features and enhancements</li>
<li>Measure platform performance and engagement</li>
</ul>

<h3>Security and Compliance</h3>
<ul>
<li>Prevent fraud and unauthorized access</li>
<li>Enforce our Terms of Service</li>
<li>Comply with legal obligations</li>
<li>Protect user rights and safety</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>3. Information Sharing and Disclosure</h2>

<h3>We Do NOT Sell Your Data</h3>
<p>VibeStore does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.</p>

<h3>Limited Sharing</h3>
<p>We may share your information only in the following circumstances:</p>

<p><strong>Service Providers:</strong> We work with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our platform:</p>
<ul>
<li>Firebase (Google) for authentication and database services</li>
<li>Analytics providers (Google Analytics) for usage analysis</li>
<li>Email service providers for newsletters and notifications</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Public Information:</strong> Information you choose to make public, such as:</p>
<ul>
<li>App listings you submit (when approved)</li>
<li>Reviews and ratings you post</li>
<li>Public profile information (if applicable)</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Legal Requirements:</strong> We may disclose information when required by law or to:</p>
<ul>
<li>Comply with legal processes or government requests</li>
<li>Enforce our Terms of Service</li>
<li>Protect our rights, property, or safety</li>
<li>Prevent fraud or security threats</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Business Transfers:</strong> In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user information may be transferred to the acquiring entity.</p>

<hr>

<h2>4. Data Security</h2>

<p>We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Encryption:</strong> All data transmission uses SSL/TLS encryption</li>
<li><strong>Authentication:</strong> Email verification required for account activation</li>
<li><strong>Access Controls:</strong> Strict database security rules and permissions</li>
<li><strong>Monitoring:</strong> Regular security audits and monitoring</li>
<li><strong>Best Practices:</strong> Following Firebase and Google Cloud security guidelines</li>
</ul>

<p>However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.</p>

<hr>

<h2>5. Your Rights and Choices</h2>

<p>You have the following rights regarding your personal information:</p>

<h3>Access and Portability</h3>
<ul>
<li>Request a copy of your personal data</li>
<li>Export your favorites, lists, and submitted apps</li>
</ul>

<h3>Correction and Deletion</h3>
<ul>
<li>Update or correct your account information</li>
<li>Request deletion of your account and associated data</li>
<li>Remove specific reviews or submissions</li>
</ul>

<h3>Communication Preferences</h3>
<ul>
<li>Unsubscribe from newsletters at any time</li>
<li>Manage email notification settings</li>
<li>Opt-out of non-essential communications</li>
</ul>

<h3>Cookie Management</h3>
<ul>
<li>Control cookie preferences in your browser</li>
<li>Opt-out of analytics tracking (may affect functionality)</li>
</ul>

<p>To exercise these rights, please contact us at vibeappstore@gmail.com or use the report feature on our platform.</p>

<hr>

<h2>6. Data Retention</h2>

<p>We retain your information for as long as necessary to:</p>
<ul>
<li>Provide our services and maintain your account</li>
<li>Comply with legal obligations</li>
<li>Resolve disputes and enforce agreements</li>
<li>Maintain business records and analytics</li>
</ul>

<p>When you delete your account:</p>
<ul>
<li>Personal information is removed from active databases</li>
<li>Public content (reviews, submissions) may be anonymized</li>
<li>Backup copies are retained for up to 90 days</li>
<li>Analytics data is aggregated and anonymized</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>7. Third-Party Services and Links</h2>

<h3>External Links</h3>
<p>Our platform contains links to third-party websites, apps, and services. We are not responsible for:</p>
<ul>
<li>Privacy practices of external sites</li>
<li>Content or accuracy of linked resources</li>
<li>Security of third-party services</li>
</ul>

<p>We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any external services you visit.</p>

<h3>Third-Party Authentication</h3>
<p>When you use Google Sign-In or similar services:</p>
<ul>
<li>We receive limited profile information (name, email)</li>
<li>Your credentials are managed by the third-party provider</li>
<li>We do not have access to your passwords</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>8. Children's Privacy</h2>

<p>VibeStore is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will promptly delete it from our systems.</p>

<p>If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at vibeappstore@gmail.com.</p>

<hr>

<h2>9. International Users</h2>

<p>VibeStore is operated from the United States. If you are accessing our platform from outside the US:</p>
<ul>
<li>Your information may be transferred to and stored in the US</li>
<li>US data protection laws may differ from your country</li>
<li>By using our services, you consent to this transfer</li>
</ul>

<p>For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), we comply with applicable data protection regulations, including GDPR principles.</p>

<hr>

<h2>10. California Privacy Rights</h2>

<p>If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):</p>
<ul>
<li>Right to know what personal information is collected</li>
<li>Right to know if personal information is sold or disclosed</li>
<li>Right to opt-out of the sale of personal information (we don't sell data)</li>
<li>Right to deletion of personal information</li>
<li>Right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights</li>
</ul>

<p>To exercise these rights, contact us at vibeappstore@gmail.com.</p>

<hr>

<h2>11. Changes to This Privacy Policy</h2>

<p>We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect:</p>
<ul>
<li>Changes in our practices or services</li>
<li>Legal or regulatory requirements</li>
<li>User feedback and industry standards</li>
</ul>

<p>When we make material changes:</p>
<ul>
<li>We will update the "Last updated" date</li>
<li>We may notify you via email or platform notification</li>
<li>Continued use of our services constitutes acceptance</li>
</ul>

<p>We encourage you to review this policy regularly to stay informed about how we protect your information.</p>

<hr>

<h2>12. Contact Us</h2>

<p>If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:</p>

<p><strong>Email:</strong> vibeappstore@gmail.com<br>
<strong>Report Feature:</strong> Use the "Report" link in our footer to submit privacy concerns</p>

<p>We will respond to your inquiry within 7 business days.</p>
