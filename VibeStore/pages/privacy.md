# Privacy Policy

<p class="policy-intro">At VibeStore, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our platform and use our services.</p>

<div class="policy-updated">Last updated: January 2025</div>

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### Personal Information
We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:
- Create an account on our platform
- Submit an app for listing
- Subscribe to our newsletter
- Leave reviews or ratings
- Contact us for support or report issues
- Participate in our community features

This information may include:
- Name and email address
- Authentication credentials (encrypted)
- Profile information
- App submission details and metadata
- User preferences and favorites

### Automatically Collected Information
When you visit VibeStore, we automatically collect certain information about your device and usage patterns:
- IP address and browser type
- Device information and operating system
- Pages visited and time spent on our platform
- Referring URLs and search terms
- Click patterns and interaction data

### Cookies and Tracking Technologies
We use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to:
- Remember your preferences and settings
- Analyze usage patterns and improve our services
- Provide personalized content and recommendations
- Measure the effectiveness of our features

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings, though some features may not function properly without cookies.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

### Service Provision
- Create and manage user accounts
- Process and display app submissions
- Enable favorites, lists, and personalization features
- Facilitate user interactions (reviews, ratings)
- Send transactional emails and notifications

### Communication
- Send newsletter updates (with your consent)
- Respond to support requests and inquiries
- Send important service announcements
- Notify you of changes to our terms or policies

### Analytics and Improvement
- Analyze user behavior and usage patterns
- Improve our search algorithms and recommendations
- Identify and fix technical issues
- Develop new features and enhancements
- Measure platform performance and engagement

### Security and Compliance
- Prevent fraud and unauthorized access
- Enforce our Terms of Service
- Comply with legal obligations
- Protect user rights and safety

---

## 3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

### We Do NOT Sell Your Data
VibeStore does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

### Limited Sharing
We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

**Service Providers:** We work with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our platform:
- Firebase (Google) for authentication and database services
- Analytics providers (Google Analytics) for usage analysis
- Email service providers for newsletters and notifications

**Public Information:** Information you choose to make public, such as:
- App listings you submit (when approved)
- Reviews and ratings you post
- Public profile information (if applicable)

**Legal Requirements:** We may disclose information when required by law or to:
- Comply with legal processes or government requests
- Enforce our Terms of Service
- Protect our rights, property, or safety
- Prevent fraud or security threats

**Business Transfers:** In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user information may be transferred to the acquiring entity.

---

## 4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

- **Encryption:** All data transmission uses SSL/TLS encryption
- **Authentication:** Email verification required for account activation
- **Access Controls:** Strict database security rules and permissions
- **Monitoring:** Regular security audits and monitoring
- **Best Practices:** Following Firebase and Google Cloud security guidelines

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## 5. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

### Access and Portability
- Request a copy of your personal data
- Export your favorites, lists, and submitted apps

### Correction and Deletion
- Update or correct your account information
- Request deletion of your account and associated data
- Remove specific reviews or submissions

### Communication Preferences
- Unsubscribe from newsletters at any time
- Manage email notification settings
- Opt-out of non-essential communications

### Cookie Management
- Control cookie preferences in your browser
- Opt-out of analytics tracking (may affect functionality)

To exercise these rights, please contact us at vibeappstore@gmail.com or use the report feature on our platform.

---

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to:
- Provide our services and maintain your account
- Comply with legal obligations
- Resolve disputes and enforce agreements
- Maintain business records and analytics

When you delete your account:
- Personal information is removed from active databases
- Public content (reviews, submissions) may be anonymized
- Backup copies are retained for up to 90 days
- Analytics data is aggregated and anonymized

---

## 7. Third-Party Services and Links

### External Links
Our platform contains links to third-party websites, apps, and services. We are not responsible for:
- Privacy practices of external sites
- Content or accuracy of linked resources
- Security of third-party services

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any external services you visit.

### Third-Party Authentication
When you use Google Sign-In or similar services:
- We receive limited profile information (name, email)
- Your credentials are managed by the third-party provider
- We do not have access to your passwords

---

## 8. Children's Privacy

VibeStore is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will promptly delete it from our systems.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at vibeappstore@gmail.com.

---

## 9. International Users

VibeStore is operated from the United States. If you are accessing our platform from outside the US:
- Your information may be transferred to and stored in the US
- US data protection laws may differ from your country
- By using our services, you consent to this transfer

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), we comply with applicable data protection regulations, including GDPR principles.

---

## 10. California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):
- Right to know what personal information is collected
- Right to know if personal information is sold or disclosed
- Right to opt-out of the sale of personal information (we don't sell data)
- Right to deletion of personal information
- Right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights

To exercise these rights, contact us at vibeappstore@gmail.com.

---

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect:
- Changes in our practices or services
- Legal or regulatory requirements
- User feedback and industry standards

When we make material changes:
- We will update the "Last updated" date
- We may notify you via email or platform notification
- Continued use of our services constitutes acceptance

We encourage you to review this policy regularly to stay informed about how we protect your information.

---

## 12. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

**Email:** vibeappstore@gmail.com  
**Report Feature:** Use the "Report" link in our footer to submit privacy concerns

We will respond to your inquiry within 7 business days.
